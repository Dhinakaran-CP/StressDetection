Zero-crossing rate (ZCR)

Acoustic Metric

Zero-crossing rate is the rate at which a signal transitions from positive to zero or negative, or vice-versa. In clinical speech analysis, a higher ZCR is often correlated with increased cognitive demand or vocal tension associated with acute stress responses.

High Range Indicative

Agitation, high-arousal states, or respiratory strain.

Clinical Correlation

Monitored during therapeutic sessions to gauge emotional regulation.
